Important: How square footage is calculated

Drywall pricing is calculated on total wall and ceiling surface area — not floor area. A 3,000 sq ft home typically has 8,000–12,000 sq ft of drywall surface depending on ceiling height, number of rooms and features like coffered ceilings.

What's Included in the Price?

A complete drywall quote from LR Contracting includes supply and delivery of drywall boards, hanging and screwing to framing, taping all joints and corners, three coats of compound with proper drying time between each, sanding between coats, and final Level 4 or Level 5 finish depending on specification.

What is not typically included: Metal framing, insulation, painting, and primer. These are quoted separately.

What Drives Drywall Costs Up on Custom Homes?

1. Ceiling Height

Standard 9 foot ceilings are straightforward. Ten, eleven and twelve foot ceilings require scaffolding, longer boards, and more labour — adding 15–25% to ceiling cost. Vaulted and cathedral ceilings add further due to angled cuts and difficult board placement.

2. Coffered and Tray Ceilings

This is where luxury custom home pricing moves significantly. A coffered ceiling requires precise framing coordination, multiple inside and outside corner beads, and extra taping detail. Expect a premium of $8–$15 per linear foot of coffered or tray ceiling detail on top of the base drywall price.

3. Level 5 Finish

A premium smooth finish requires a full skim coat over every surface — not just over joints. It adds roughly $0.75–$1.50 per sq ft but is the only appropriate finish grade for custom homes where gloss or semi-gloss paint will be used.

⚠️ Be cautious of very low quotes

If a drywall quote in Oakville is significantly below market, ask detailed questions. Low quotes often mean thinner boards, fewer coats of compound, skipped sanding steps, or crews unfamiliar with Level 5 work. Redoing poor drywall after painting costs far more than paying the right price the first time.
